# Sourcing, access and ethics — presenter cheat sheet

## Useful places to find datasets
- [data.gov](https://data.gov/) — US government datasets; inspect each record's provenance and terms.
- [UCI Machine Learning Repository](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/) — traditional ML benchmarks.
- [Kaggle Datasets](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets) — competition/community datasets; licenses vary.
- [Hugging Face Datasets](https://huggingface.co/datasets) — modern ML datasets and dataset cards.
- [Common Crawl](https://commoncrawl.org/) — raw/processed web crawls.
- [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/) — digitized ebooks.

## Questions to ask on every dataset slide
1. Who collected it, and for what original purpose?
2. Who or what is missing? What labels or proxies were selected?
3. What permission applies to collection, redistribution and reuse?
4. What changed when the dataset was repurposed?

## Key distinctions
- **Publicly accessible ≠ public domain ≠ openly licensed ≠ consent for every use.**
- **Free to obtain ≠ free to process:** storage, annotation, cleaning and compute can be expensive.
- **Anonymized ≠ impossible to reidentify:** Netflix Prize illustrates linkage risk.
- **A benchmark ≠ the real world:** MNIST and ImageNet simplify broad, messy tasks.
